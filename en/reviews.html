Practice stories

“I am 45 and at the first signs of menopause. I experienced exhaustion, helplessness, apathy, irritability and dissatisfaction with myself. I felt out of control because of hormonal changes and frequent hot flashes throughout my body. This was a difficult psychological period. Besides medication, I needed physical practice to improve my mental, emotional and physical state. This led me to an inspiring and knowledgeable yoga teacher, Alina, who works with women's health through powerful practices, including yoga for women experiencing menopause. The process was deeply immersive, gentle and inspiring. I enjoyed every movement, felt my femininity, inner beauty and tenderness, and experienced so much love and care for myself during practice. I left with a beautiful mood, renewed energy, desire to move forward and acceptance of myself. Alina, thank you for your sincere desire to help, your experience and knowledge, attention, care and powerful energy. Thank you for being willing to work with such complex women's experiences. You are amazing!”
